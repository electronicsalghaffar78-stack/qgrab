Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions.

Is QGrab free to use?

Yes. QGrab is completely free — no account, no sign-up and no software installation. The site is supported by the ads you see on the page.

Is it legal to download videos?

Please download only videos that you own, that you created yourself, or that you have the owner's permission to save — for personal, offline use. Copyrighted content belongs to its creators; downloading and re-uploading other people's work without permission can violate copyright law and the platform's terms.

Which quality should I choose?

Formats marked "with sound" include the audio in a single file — pick those for normal watching. Higher numbers (1080p, 720p) mean sharper video and bigger files; 360p is small and fast. "Audio" options give you just the sound, which is handy for music and lectures.

Why did my link fail?

Private, age-restricted or region-locked videos may not work, and some platforms change their systems from time to time. Check that the link opens in your browser, then try again.

Does it work on phones?

Yes — QGrab is built mobile-first and works in the browser on Android and iPhone, as well as on tablets and computers.

Install The QGrab App

QGrab works as an app too — install it on your phone for one-tap access from your home screen, or use it right here in any PC browser. No app store needed.

  • Android (Chrome): tap the browser menu (⋮) → "Install app" or "Add to Home screen".
  • iPhone (Safari): tap the Share button → "Add to Home Screen".
  • PC (Chrome/Edge): click the install icon in the address bar, or just keep using the website — it works the same.
