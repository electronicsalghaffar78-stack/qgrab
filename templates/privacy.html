Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

QGrab is built to be simple and private: there are no accounts, no sign-ups, and no database. This policy explains, in plain language, what happens to your information when you use the site.

What we do not collect or store

  • We do not store the links you paste. A link is processed in memory only to fetch the video's formats and deliver your download, and is then discarded.
  • We do not store downloaded files. Files pass through our server only for the moment needed to send them to your device; they are not kept, catalogued or shared.
  • We do not ask for personal information. There is no registration, so we never collect your name, email address or password.

Standard server logs

Like almost every website, our hosting provider may keep standard technical logs for security and reliability — for example your IP address, browser type, and the time of a request. These logs are generated automatically by the hosting platform, are kept only for a limited time, and are not used by us to identify you or build profiles.

Cookies and the app

QGrab itself does not use tracking cookies. If you install QGrab as an app, your browser stores the app's own files on your device so it opens quickly — this stays on your device and sends us nothing. Advertising shown on the site is served by third-party ad networks, which may use their own cookies under their own privacy policies.

Third-party platforms

When you paste a link, our server contacts the platform that hosts the video (such as YouTube or TikTok) to fetch it. Those platforms have their own privacy policies, which apply to their services.
